Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

For Residents

How do I report waste?

From your dashboard, click the "Report Waste" button. Fill in the details about the waste type and weight, capture your location, and upload at least one clear photo of the waste. Our AI will analyze the image to help classify it.

How does the reward system work?

You receive a small, instant reward of 10 points for every valid report you submit. You will receive a much larger "Impact Bonus" after the waste is collected and successfully processed by one of our partner vendors. The bonus amount depends on the energy generated or CO2 reduced.

Can I cancel a waste report?

Yes, you can cancel a report from your dashboard as long as a collector has not been dispatched for pickup (i.e., its status is not "awaiting_collection" or later). This ensures our collectors' time is not wasted.

For Collectors

How do I see my assigned pickups?

All your assigned pickups are listed in the "Task List" on your dashboard. You can also see them plotted on the interactive map. New tasks will appear automatically as they are assigned to you.

How do I update the status of a pickup?

Click on a task to open its details. When you are at the bin's location, you can click "Mark Collected". After you have transported the waste to the vendor's facility, open the details again and click "Mark Delivered".

For Vendors

How do I find waste to process?

On your dashboard, the "Available Bins" section shows all nearby smart bins that have reached a high fill level and contain waste types you specialize in. You can filter this list to find the materials you need.

What happens after I request a bin?

Once you request a bin, our system automatically assigns the closest available collector to pick it up and deliver it to your facility. You can track the status of this request on your dashboard.
