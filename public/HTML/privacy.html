Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2025

1. Introduction

EcoChain ("us", "we", or "our") operates the EcoChain platform (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

  • Personal Data: While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to: email address, full name, and phone number.
  • Usage Data: We may also collect information on how the Service is accessed and used. This Usage Data may include information such as your computer's IP address, browser type, and browser version.
  • Location Data: We collect precise location data when you submit a waste report to identify the location of the waste and to assign the nearest collector. Collectors' locations are tracked while they are on duty to enable proximity-based task assignment.
  • Media Data: We collect the images of waste that you upload as part of a waste report for the purpose of AI-based classification.

3. How We Use Your Information

EcoChain uses the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service.
  • To manage your account and provide you with customer support.
  • To facilitate the core functionality of waste collection, such as assigning collectors to bins and providing vendors with information about available waste.
  • To operate and calculate the reward system.
  • To monitor the usage of our Service and improve its functionality.

4. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete the information we have on you. You can do this at any time by accessing your account settings or contacting us directly.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
